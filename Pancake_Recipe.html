Pancakes

Pancakes are a classic, quick, and delicious breakfast loved by people of all ages. Soft, fluffy, and lightly golden, pancakes are easy to prepare using simple ingredients available at home. They can be enjoyed with honey, maple syrup, chocolate sauce, fruits, or butter, making them perfect for both kids and adults. This recipe is ideal for beginners and can be made in just a few minutes.

Ingredients

  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Coco powder

Instructions

  1. Mix flour, milk, sugar, baking powder, and a pinch of salt into a smooth batter.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan and lightly grease it.
  3. Pour the batter, cook until bubbles form, then flip and cook till golden.
  4. Serve hot with honey, syrup, or fruits.
